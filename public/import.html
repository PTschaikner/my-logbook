Bring the crags and routes from your old single-file version into the database. This is a one-time step.

  1. Open your old Crag Log, open the browser console, and run:
    copy(localStorage.craglog2)
  2. Paste the copied text below.
  3. Either push straight to your live API, or generate SQL to run with wrangler.
